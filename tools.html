Rule of thumb: at the same power, doubling the voltage halves the current — and cuts cable heat (I²R) to a quarter. That is why bigger DC systems move to 48 V and EV packs run at 400–800 V.

How these calculators work

Transparent assumptions

Every default comes from public sources (US DOE AFDC, fueleconomy.gov, NREL PVWatts, EIA, Battery University). Change any input to match your situation.

Estimates, not sign-off

Real charging curves vary by car and temperature; real solar output varies by shading and tilt. Use results for planning, then confirm with a qualified installer.

Private by design

All maths runs in your browser. Nothing you type into a calculator is sent anywhere unless you choose to request a quote.
