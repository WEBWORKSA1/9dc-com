How judging works

  1. Safety (30%)Proper fusing, cable sizing, ventilation and enclosures. Unsafe builds are not eligible.
  2. Design & documentation (30%)A clear parts list, diagram and the numbers behind your choices.
  3. Value (20%)Capability for the money and smart re-use.
  4. Craft & story (20%)Workmanship, photos and what you learned.

Rules in brief

  • Free to enter; no purchase necessary. One entry per person per month.
  • You must own the photos and the build (or have permission).
  • Entrants must be 18+ and the contest is void where prohibited.
  • Work on mains or high-voltage DC must be done or inspected by a qualified person where your local law requires.
  • By entering you allow 9DC to feature your photos and description with credit. You keep ownership.
  • Winners are announced in the first week of the following month and contacted by email; prizes are paid by digital transfer.
