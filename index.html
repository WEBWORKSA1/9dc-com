FAQ

Quick answers

Why does DC fast charging slow down after 80%?

As a lithium-ion cell fills, the car's battery management system lowers the current to limit heat and prevent lithium plating, so power tapers. In practice the last 20% can take about as long as going from 10% to 80%. Try it in the fast-charge calculator.

Are solar panels AC or DC?

Panels produce direct current. A string inverter or microinverters convert it to alternating current for your home and the grid. Batteries store DC, which is why where you connect them matters — see DC- vs AC-coupled systems.

Is 12 V, 24 V or 48 V better for an off-grid system?

Higher voltage means lower current for the same power, so thinner, cheaper cable and lower losses. 12 V suits small van and boat systems; 24 V suits mid-size RVs; 48 V is the norm for cabins and homes with inverters above roughly 3 kW.

How accurate are the calculators?

They use widely published engineering assumptions and are good for planning and comparing options. Real results depend on your specific vehicle, roof, climate and equipment — always confirm designs with a qualified professional.
